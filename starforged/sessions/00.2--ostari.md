> I came into Starforged with a vague plan for a group of three ship-mates who will be just 
> starting out their time together. A Researcher/explorer from a utopian/ideallist faction who 
> escaped with a ship as the lone survivor from a research mission. They have sold their grand 
> vison of a better Forge to the mechanic who fixed up the ship, and recruited a pilot.
>
> Starforged can be played with a single character, but I'd like to have group dynamics and a
> range of character abilities for more believable problem-solving.
> 
> As the seed around which the group has grown, and 'owner' of the ship I'll build up the 
> researcher first. Characters in Starforged have five basic stats (which come later in the 
> process) and some status trackers, but the majority of their specialisation and quirks come from 
> their assets. Each of these come with a starting ability, and two abilities unlockable with 
> experience.
> 
> The group has a single command vehicle asset - their starship, which can have multiple module 
> assets fitted and/or house support vehicle assets. Individual characters have path assetss 
> representing things inherent to them, background, interests, training, skills, powers, etc. 
> They might be accompanied by a companion asset. There are also Deed assets that are 
> rewards/mitigations for notable story events and can't be taken until their requirements are met.
> 
> Character generation has 11 steps in the book, but some can be grouped:
> 1) Theme: Pick two starting path assets
> 2) Background: Sketch a backstory and write a background vow as the primary motivation/goal 
>    for the character
> 3) Starship: Describe the ship and its origins, give it a name
> 4) Finalise: Pick another path or companion asset for yourself, or contribute a module or 
>    support vehicle to the ship; assign your stats
> 5) Vision: Describe the character and their gear, give them a name.

## Theme

> There are 46 paths to choose from which is a little overwhelming, but there is also a table of 
> 20 archetypes. I'm going to pick one that seems to fit, then consider if I want to swap one 
> out. For our researcher, Exobiologist seems to fit which gives the LORE HUNTER and NATURALIST 
> paths.
>
> ```asset
> name: Lore Hunter
> type: Path
> startingAbility: |
>   When you <u>Swear an Iron Vow</u> (formidable or greater) to recover valuable knowledge or an 
>   extraordinary relic, reroll any dice. When you <u>Reach a Milestone</u> in the pursuit of that 
>   quest, take +2 momentum. When you <u>Fulfill Your Vow</u> and score a hit, also mark 2 ticks on 
>   your discoveries legacy track. 
> ```
> 
> ```asset
> name: Naturalist
> type: Path
> startingAbility: |
>   When you <u>Secure an Advantage</u> or <u>Gather Information</u> using your knowledge of 
>   lifeforms or planetside ecosystems, add +1 and take +1 momentum on a hit. On a strong hit with a
>   match, you also confirm an obscure theory or reveal a surprising aspect of the encounter; mark 1
>   tick on your discoveries legacy track.
> ```
> 
> For the utopian/ideallist faction, I'm thinking I might have them researching for the Terran 
> Apothecary^[A collective of medics who've defected from corporate employment, see 
> [Medicine](../00.1--truths#truth-medicine) in part 1.]. If so being a HEALER might fit better than LORE HUNTER.
> 
> ```asset
> name: Healer
> type: Path
> startingAbility: |
>   When you give medical care to <u>Heal</u> yourself or another character, add +1. If you are
>   treating someone other than yourself, take +1 spirit or +1 momentum on a hit.
> ```
> 
> I want NATURALIST either way, so it's a choice between LORE HUNTER and HEALER. As a LORE HUNTER 
> they're driven by unlocking the secrets of the Forge to better its inhabitants and have fallen 
> in with the Apothecaries due to aligned ideals. As a HEALER, they are an Apothecary first and 
> foremost, with a drive to expand and improve the organisation. I'd be happy with either so I'll 
> let the dice choose, with a 50/50 split [d100:15] - a LORE HUNTER it is. I suspect they'll pick
> up HEALER in play, but that will come later.

## Background

> Starforged is designed that you start with a fairly blank slate. Something has happened to cut 
> you off from your home and previous relationships. Of the suggestions: "You are the sole survivor 
> of an attack or calamity" fits best. Given the clandestine nature of the Terran Apothecary, 
> lets say they were a disillusioned corporate university drop out, recruited whilst researching 
> their final year project. 
> 
> Let's also envision out first corporation. AgriFlora, has a monopoly on food production, 
> specialising in genetically engineered crops, and has a bunch of terraformed farm worlds with 
> terrible working conditions. They host a university which specialises in biotech and doubles 
> as their R&D department.
> 
> They were on their first mission with the Terran Apothecary, and had taken what was to become 
> their ship on a solo recon mission. They came back to find their research base under attack by 
> unknown assailants. There was a dangerous escape whilst they were pursued, and some unknown 
> precursor tech was activated and managed to use non-standard drifts to evade capture. The rest 
> of the details can be built out in flashbacks as they become relevant.
> 
> Finally, we need a background Iron Vow. This will be epic (i.e. requires a total of 40 
> progress to fill the track - the longest in the game), and should take most of the campaign to 
> complete. I have three ideas here:
> 1) The attack on the research base was part of something much bigger - and they vow to get 
>    justice.
> 2) Going all in on LORE HUNTER and discovering and recreating some form of precursor biotech
> 3) Reconnect with the Terran Apothecary, and be material in them setting up a civilisation 
>    that can rival the corps.
> 
> I went into this planning that the vow would be the justice, but given that this is a pillar 
> of defining the character, idealism seems more appropriate. The discovery path seems really 
> hard to pull off in a satisfying way. The background is too loose to support interesting 
> discovery arcs that feel realistic and engaging. 
> 
> ```vow
> level: epic
> slug: ostari-background
> character: Ostari
> tags: ['background']
> vow: |
>   I vow to realise the Terran Apothecaries vision of a safe, equitable society separate
>   and protected from corporate control.
> progress: 0
> ```
> 
> I can still investigate the attack, and swear vows of justice in play. If it turns out to have 
> been corporate, it may even synergise with the background vow.
